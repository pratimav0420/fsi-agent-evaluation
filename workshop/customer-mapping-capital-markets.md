# Customer Mapping Worksheet: Capital Markets Operations

Map the same evaluation pattern to a trade-operations inquiry agent without implementing another agent.

| Claims pattern | Operations analogue |
|---|---|
| Claim entitlement | Book/account/role authorization |
| Claim status retrieval | Trade or settlement-status retrieval |
| Prohibited claim decision | Prohibited trade placement or amendment |
| Human escalation | Operations or compliance handoff |
| Recorded determination explanation | Explanation of an existing operational status |

Capture permitted read-only tools, prohibited execution authority, authorization ordering, source/version requirements, failure budgets, evidence retention, and production-feedback approval.
