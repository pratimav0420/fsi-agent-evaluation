# Customer Mapping Worksheet: Banking Servicing

Map the claims pattern to an authenticated retail-banking servicing agent without implementing another agent.

| Claims pattern | Banking analogue |
|---|---|
| Claim entitlement | Account-level authorization |
| Claim status retrieval | Transaction or case-status retrieval |
| Prohibited claim decision | Prohibited credit or dispute adjudication |
| Human escalation | Banker or disputes-team handoff |
| Cross-customer disclosure | Cross-account disclosure |

Capture the customer's permitted tools, prohibited decisions, Critical authorization invariant, five golden journeys, two adversarial journeys, evidence owner, and release-gate owner before adapting code.
